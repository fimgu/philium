# grill-me

Challenge the user's reasoning before acting on a plan.

1. Before editing anything, restate the plan in one or two sentences.
2. Ask the sharpest open questions, one at a time: the goal behind the request, unstated assumptions, simpler alternatives, failure modes, how success will be verified, and what is out of scope.
3. Push back when an answer is vague or contradicts earlier facts. Name the risk plainly.
4. Stop grilling once the plan is concrete and the user confirms. Summarize the agreed plan, then act.
5. Never grill trivial, reversible requests or explicit "just do it" instructions.
